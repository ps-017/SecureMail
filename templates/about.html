About SecureMail

Defensive Email Analysis & Threat Detection Platform

SecureMail is a lightweight, protocol-level email forensic utility designed to deconstruct MIME messages, evaluate authentication signatures, and uncover evasive phishing techniques.

Built to bridge the gap between complex enterprise secure email gateways and everyday users, SecureMail provides transparent, zero-dependency header analysis without retaining or exposing user communications.

System Capabilities
RFC 5322 MIME Decomposition Cryptographic Auth Verification (SPF, DKIM, DMARC) Domain & Envelope Alignment Auditing Heuristic Threat & Social Engineering Detection In-Memory Threat Evaluation

Why SecureMail Was Built

Deconstructing the structural asymmetries that attackers exploit in modern messaging.

Vulnerability

The Blind Spot

Standard email clients display only the superficial display name and subject line, completely concealing low-level routing manipulations, unauthorized relay IP hops, and spoofed envelope return paths from recipient view.

Attack Surface

The Problem

Modern credential phishing and Business Email Compromise (BEC) routinely bypass perimeter spam filters by abusing legitimate third-party relay infrastructure, unaligned sender addresses, or deceptive typosquatted domains.

Our Defense

The Solution

SecureMail delivers instant, transparent header and content decomposition. It interrogates protocol records, scores multi-vector deception cues, and isolates threats directly without requiring complex enterprise email gateways.

Under the Hood: Detection Pipeline

Deterministic heuristic methodology evaluating messages across multiple verification layers.

LAYER 01

MIME & Protocol Parsing

Built on native Python RFC 5322 parsing standards. Extracts multi-part structures, decodes nested payloads, and isolates raw transfer encodings without external parsing overhead.

LAYER 02

Multi-Vector Risk Scoring

Employs a deterministic heuristic engine that inspects envelope mismatches (From vs. Return-Path), validates Authentication-Results records, and analyzes urgency triggers.

LAYER 03

IoC & Payload Extraction

Systematically parses hidden hyperlink redirects, identifies typosquatted domains or raw IP hosts, and flags high-risk attachment extensions (.exe, .scr, .iso, .bat).

Security Architecture

Privacy First: In-Memory Analysis

Zero Data Persistence

Emails and .eml files are processed strictly in volatile memory (RAM) and immediately discarded post-analysis.

No Database or Telemetry Logging

No user content, headers, recipient lists, or extracted links are ever written to disk or third-party servers.

Local-First Verification

Analysis executes entirely on the local application instance, preventing accidental credential or sensitive data leakage.
